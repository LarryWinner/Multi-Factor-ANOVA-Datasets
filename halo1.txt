Dataset:  halo1.csv

Source:  Landy and Sigall (1974). "Beauty is Talent: Task Evaluation
as a Function of the Performer's Physical Attraction," Journal of
Personality and Social Psychology, 29:299-304.

Description: Two Factor experiment to assess if appearance effects
judgment of student's essay. Appearance (Attractive/Control/Unattractive)
and Essay Quality (Good/Poor) are factors (Pictures given with essay, except
in control). Response: Grade on essay. Data simulated to match their means
and standard deviations.

Variable Names
essayQual       /* 1=Good, 2=Poor  */
picture  /*  1= Attractive, 2=Control, 3=Unattractive  */
score
