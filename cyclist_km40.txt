Dataset: cyclist_km40.csv

Source: M.F. Smith, R.C.R. Davison, J. Balmer, S.R. Bird (2001).
"Reliability of Mean Power Recorded During Indoor and Outdoor Self-Paced
40 km Cycling Time-Trials," International Journal of Sports Medicine,
Vol. 22, pp. 270-274.

Description: Performance Times and power output for 8 non-elite, endurance-trained,
competitive cyclists on 3 trials each on indoor and outdoor tracks.

Variable Names:
cyclist (1-8)
trial (1-3)
venue (1=indoor, 2=outdoor)
km40time   (minutes)
km40power  (watts)
