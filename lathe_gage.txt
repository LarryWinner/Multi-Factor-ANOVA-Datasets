Dataset: lathe_gage.csv

Source: C. Saikaew (2018)> "An Implementation of Measurement System Analysis
for Assessment of Machine and Part Variations in Turning Opereation,"
Measurement, Vol. 118, pp. 246-252.

Description: Results of measurement experiment measuring 10 
randomly selected parts
with 3 randomly selected CNC lathe machines. 3 Trials per part/machine.
Trial is not a factor in the model.

Variable names:
machine
trial
partNum
diameter (Y)
