Dataset: spirits_alccont.csv

Source: A.S. Oliveira, F.M. Dalla Nora, R.O. Mello, P.A. Mello, B. Tischer,
A.B. Costa, J.S. Barin (2017). "One-Shot, Reagent-Free Determination of the
Alcohol Content of Distilled Beverages by Thermal Infrared Enthalpimetry,"
Talanta, Vol. 171, pp. 335-340.

Description: Measurements of alcohol content in 3 types of distilled spirits
(Vodka, Whiskey, Cachaca) and 3 brands nested within each spirit by
thermal infrared enthalpimetry (TIE), with 24 replicates per brand.
Data simulated to match means/SDs reported.

Variable names:
spiritType (1=Vodka, 2=Whiskey. 3=Cachaca)
brandSprt  (1-3=Vodka brands, 4-6=Whiskey brands, 7-9=Cachaca brands)
labelAC    (label % alcohol)
alcCntnt   (measured % alcohol)
