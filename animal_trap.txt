Source: animal_trap.csv

Source: S.R. Cook and G. Proulx (1989). "Mechanical Evaluation and 
Performance Improvement of the Rotating Jaw Conibear 120 Trap," Journal of
Testing and Evaluation, Vol. 17, No. 3, pp. 190-195

Description: Experiment comparing 8 models of animal traps, 3 traps per
model, with 10 replicates per trap. 
Response: Trap momentum at HDISP (when both jaws displaced halfway)
Data generated to match statistics reported in paper.
Trap Models: 1=C120, 2=C126, 3=C120 Mark III, 4=C120 Mark II,
   5=C120 Mark V, 6=C120 Mark IV, 7=C120 Mark VII, 8=C120 Mark VI 

Variable Names:
trapGrp    (Combination of model and trap w/in model)
model
trapModel
momentum   (kg m/s)
