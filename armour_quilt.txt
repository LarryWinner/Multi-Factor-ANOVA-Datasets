Source: D.J. Carr, C. Lankester, A.Peare, N. Fibri, N. Gridley (2012).
"Does Quilting Improve the Fragment Protective Performance of Body Armour?,"
Textile Research Journal, Vol. 82, #9, pp. 883-888.

to instrument error. Data simulated to match mean, sd, sample size by treatment.
