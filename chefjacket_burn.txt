Dataset: chefjacket_burn.csv

Source: S. Devarajan, R.H. McQueen, S. Wen (2017). "Can Common Finishing
Treatments Used in Chef Jacket Fabrics Improve Protection Against Scald
Injury?," Fashion and Textiles, 4:19, pp. 1-15.

Description: Three-Way factorial experiment with factors:
Finish: 1=Regular, 2=Water, 3=Soil, 4=Teflon
Structure: 1=Plain, 2=Twill
Number of layers: 1,2,3
Responses: Absorbed Energy (kJ/m^2) at Upper, Middle, and Lower Sensors
Data generated to match means/SDs (no attempt at multivariate responses)

Variable Names:
finish
structure
layers
trt      (combination of factor levels)
absEnrgU
absEnrgM
absEnrgL
