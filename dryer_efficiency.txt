Dataset: dryer_efficiency.dat

Source: W.M. To, T.W. Yu, T.M. Lai, S.P. Li (2007). "Characterization of
Commercial Clothes Dryers Based on Energy-Efficiency Analysis,"
International Journal of Clothing Science and Technology,
Vol. 19, #5, pp. 270-290.

Desription: 3 Replicates each of 3 clothing categories (1=Towels,
2=Jeans, 3=Thermal Clothing) for 4 dryer types (1=Electric Dryer, 
2=Bi-directional Electric dryer, 3=Town Gas-Fired Dryer, 4=LPG-Fired dryer)

Responses = energy effectiveness (kWh/kg) and standardized cycle time (min)

Variables/Columns
Clothing Category     8
Dryer Type          16
Energy Effectiveness   18-24
Standardized Cycle Time  28-32
