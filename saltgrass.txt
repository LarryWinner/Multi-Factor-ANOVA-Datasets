Dataset:  saltgrass.dat

Source: A. Bustan, D. Pasternak, I. Pirigova, M. Durikov, T. Devries, S. El-Meccawi, A. Degen (2005).
"Evaluation of Saltgrass as a Fodder Crop for Livestock," Journal of the Science of Food and
Agriculture, Vol. 85, pp. 2077-2084

Description: Ash content (g/kg DM) of saltgrass for 16 locations at 4 salinity 
levels (1.5, 10, 30, 50 dS/m), with 6 replicates per treatment combination. Data simulated
to match results in Table 4.
