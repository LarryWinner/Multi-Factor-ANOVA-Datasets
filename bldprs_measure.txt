Dataset:  bldprs_measure.csv

Source: J.M. Bland and D.G. Altman (1999). "Measuring Agreement in Method
Comparison Studies," Statistical Methods in Medical Research, Vol. 8,
pp. 135-160.

Description: Blood Pressure measurements on 85 subjects, with 3 methods,
and 3 replicates per method per subject. Methods:
Experienced Rater J using sphygmomanometer: J1, J2, J3
Experienced Rater R using sphygmomanometer: R1, R2, R3
Semi-automated blood pressure monitor: S1, S2, S3
Subject ID is labelled: subj_id
