Dataset  beerhead_halflife.dat

Source: J.R. Hudson (1968). "Institute of Brewing: Analysis Committee
Measurement of Head-Retention in Bottled Beer," Journal of the Institute of
Brewing, Vol. 74, Issue 3, pp. 275-285.

Description: Beer head half lifes for 5 beer brands, 5 laboratories,
2 types of carborundum (distributed by maker, purchased in market),
with 4 replicates per combination.

Variables/Columns
Laboratory     8
Carborundum    16    /* 1=Distributed, 2=Purchased  */
Beer           24
Foam half-life (seconds)   28-32
