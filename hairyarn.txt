Dataset: hairyarn.dat

Source: X. Wang, W. Huang, X. Huang (1999). "Effect of Test Speed and Twist
Level on the Hairiness of Worsted Yarns," Textile Research Journal,
Volume 69, pp.889-892.

Description: Hairiness index scores for yarns at 3 test speeds of
3 different twists, spun on 6 bobbins. 

Variables/Columns
Twist Level  8  /* 1=373tpm, 2=563, 3=665   *?
Test Speed   16  /* 1=25m/min, 2=100, 3=400 */
Bobbin Number  24
Hairiness Index (x100)   29-32
