Dataset: diaper_landfill.csv

Source: K.L. Light, D.G. Chirmuley, R.K. Ham (1995).
"A Laboratory Study of the Compaction Characteristics of Disposable
Diapers in a Landfill," Resources, Conservation and Recycling,
Vol. 13, pp. 89-96.

Description: Results of experiment involving 8 diaper mixtures, at
4 pressures (14.7, 34.4, 49.1, and 78.6 psi). Authors fit 2-Way ANOVA 
with 1 observation per treatment.
Response = Density of compacted refuse excluding the weight of diapers (lbs/yd^3)

Variable Names:
diaperMix
pressure
density
