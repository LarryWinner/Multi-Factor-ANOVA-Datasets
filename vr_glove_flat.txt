Dataset:  vr_glove_flat.csv

Source: R. Thilakarathna and M. Phlernjai (2025).
"Finger Tracking for Wearable VR Glove Using Flexible Rack Mechanism,"
Virtual Reality & Intelligent Hardware, Vol. 7, #1, pp.1-25.

Description: Results of a Gage Repeatabilty & Reproducuibilty Study
with 5 Subjects (Random), 5 fingers (Fixed) and 5 replicates.
Authors performed Average/Range GR&R Analysis

Variable Names:
participant  (1-5)
finger (1=thumb, 2=index, 3=middle, 4=ring, 5=pinky)
replicate   (not a factor)
flatTest    (response, technically a mean of 10 measurements)
