Dataset:  lifting_task.dat

Source: S. Singh and S. Kumar (2012). "Factorial Analysis of Lifting Task
to Determine the Effect of Different Parameters and Interactions," Journal
of Manufacturing Technology Managenet, Vol. 23, #7, pp. 947-953.

Description: Experiment conducted to determine relationships among
factors (object weight =10,15,20; twisting angle = 0,30,45; lifiting
frequency=2,3,4) on lifting index (at origin and destination). High lifting 
index is associated with higher back pain.

Variables/Columns
object weight in kg    7-8
twising angle in degrees   15-16
lifting frequency per minute   24
Lifting Index at Origin    28-32
Lifting Index at destination  36-40
