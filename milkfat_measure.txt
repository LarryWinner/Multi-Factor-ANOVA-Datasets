Dataset:  milkfat_measure.csv

Source: J.M. Bland and D.G. Altman (1999). "Measuring Agreement in Method
Comparison Studies," Statistical Methods in Medical Research, Vol. 8,
pp. 135-160.

Description: Human milk fat content measurements on 45 subjects, with 2 methods.
Methods:
Glycerol released by enzymic hydolysis of triglycerides (triglycerides)
Standard Gerber method (gerber)
Subject ID is labelled: subj_id
