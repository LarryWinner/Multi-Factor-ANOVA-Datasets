Dataset: woolfrict1.dat

Source: J. Lindberg(1953). "Relationship Between Various Surface Properties
of Wool Fibers: Part II: Frictional Properties," Textile Research Journal, 
Vol. 23, pp. 225-237.

Description: Results of Experiment measuring top shrinkage under 4 
treatments (untreated, alcoholic potash (15sec), alcoholic potash (4min),
alcoholic potash (15 min)), measured after 7 levels of numbers of revolutions
(200:1400 by 200), over 4 experimental runs (blocks). Conducted as
a split-plot experiment with whole plot factor=treatment, sub-plot factor
= # revolutions in 4 blocks (runs)

Variables/Columns
Run    8
Treatment     16   /* 1=untrt, 2=ac(15s), 3=ac(4m), 4=ac(15m)  */
Number of Revolutions   21-24
Top Shrinkage     29-32
