Dataset:  coastguard_ethics.dat

Source: R.D. White, Jr. (1999). "Are Women More Ethical? Recent
Findings on the Effects of Gender Under Moral Development," Journal
of Public Administration Research and Theory, Vol. 9, #3, pp.459-471.

Description: Ethics scores for a sample of members of the U.S. Coast
Guard by Gender (1=Male, 2=Female) and Rank (1=Officer, 2=Enlisted).
Data simulated to match cell means and SDs.

Variables/Columns
Gender           8
Rank            16
Ethics Score    18-24
