Dataset: meadowfoamflakes.csv

Source: K.D. Carlson, B.S. Phillips, T.A. Isbell, T.C. Nelsen (1998). "Extaction of Oil from Meadowfoam Flakes," 
Journal of the American Oil Chemists' Society," Vol. 75, pp. 1429-1436.

Description: Experiment with 3 factors relating 3 factors to oil extraction from meadowfoam flakes.
Factor A: Roll opening (inches/1000): 5, 13, 20
Factor B: Seed Moisture (%): 9, 12, 15
Factor C: Seed temperature (F): 65, 190, 210
Responses: Flake thickness (in/1000)    Oil extracted (% dry basis)

Variable Names:
rollOpen
seedMoist
seedTemp
rep    (replicate)
flkThck
oilXtrct
