Dataset:  mastersy.dat

Source:  www.masters.org

Description: Scores in the Masters golf tournament (unit=player /year) for
all golfers completing all 4 rounds in the tournament (excludes 
rounds among withdrawals, disqualifications, and those missing
the cut). 1934-2004. No tourney held in 1943-1945.

Variables/Columns
finish position   7-8
golfer 17-49
round1 score  53-54
round2 score  61-62
round3 score  69-70
round4 score  77-78
total score   84-86
score wrt par   92-94
year 99-102
