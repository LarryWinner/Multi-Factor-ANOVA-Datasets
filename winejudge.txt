Dataset:  winejudge.dat

Sources: O. Ashenfelter and K. Storchmann (2012). "Editorial: The Judgement
of Princeton and Other Articles," Journal of Wine Economics, Vol. 7,
#2, pp. 139-142.

Quandt, R.E. (2012). Tasting Report No. 161. Liquid Assets. Online at
http://www.liquidasset.com/report161.html

Descrption: Results of 9 judges rating 10 white wines and 10 red wines
on a 20 point scale.

Judges: 1=J-M. Cardebat, 2=T. Colman, 3=J. Foy, 4=O. Gergaud,
5=R. Hodgson, 6=L. Murphy, 7=D. Meulders, 8=J. Rayvis, 9=F. Schott
