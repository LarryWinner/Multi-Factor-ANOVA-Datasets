Dataset: koji.csv

Source: A. Iwami, Y. Kajiwara, H. Takashita, and T. Omori (2005). "effect
of the Variety of Barley and Pearling Rate on the Quality of Shochu Koji,"
Journal of the Institute of Brewing, Vol. 111, #3, pp. 309-315.

Description: 2-factor study (1 rep per trt, average of 3 measurements) 
relating various measures to 
Barley Variety (1=Stirling, 2=Schooner, 3=Arapiles, 4=Grimmett, 5=Nishinohohi) 
Pearling Rate (50%, 60%, 70%, 80%). 
Authors tested variables with *.

Variables/Columns
barley      (Barley Variety)       
pearlRt     (Pearling Rate) 
totStarch   (Total Starch)
nitrogen      
phosphorous   
potassium      
magnesium   
calcium  
mycelia     * (mug/g-drykoji)/100    
alphAmyl    * (alpha-amylase)  
glucoAmyl   * (glucoamylase)  
acidProt    * (acid protease)
acidCarb    * (acid carboxypeptidase)
acidity     * 
glucose 
maltose
