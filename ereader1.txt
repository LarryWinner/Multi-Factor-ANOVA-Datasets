Dataset:  ereader1.csv

Source: P.-C. Chang, S.-Y. Chou, K.-K. Shieh (2013). "Reading Performance
and Visual Fatigue When Using Electronic Displays in Long-Duration
Reading Tasks Under Various Lighting Conditions," Displays, Vol. 34,
pp. 208-214.

Description: Reading Times for 3 devices and 4 lighting conditions (200Lx,
500Lx, 1000Lx, 1500Lx). Completely Randomized design with 5 subjects per
treatment. Data simulated to obtain same ANOVA and treatment means.


Variables/Columns
device     
illum
readTime  (seconds)
