Dataset: shampoo1.csv

Source: J. Lenoir, I. Claerhout, P. Kestelyn, A. Klomp, J-P. Remon, E. Adriens (2011).
"The slug mucosal irritation (SMI) assay: Development of a screening tool
for the evaluation of ocular discomfort caused by shampoos," Toxicology in Vitro, Vol. 25, pp. 1919-1925.

Description: Experiment conducted to measure SMI in 8 shampoos in 8 runs, with 3 replicates per shampoo/run
Response: SIB (Stinging, Itching, and Burning score when applied to slugs, YUCK!)
Shampoos: 
1  Water (Negative Control)
2  Artificial Tear
3  Shampoo A
4  Shampoo B
5  Shampoo C
6  Shampoo D
7  Shampoo E
8  1% Benzalkonium Chloride (BAC, Positive Control)
Data generated to match Shampoo/Run Means and SDs

Variable Names:
Shampoo (Character)
Type.ID  
Run
SIB
