Dataset: ropestrength.dat

Source: S. Cutler and J. Lebaron (1992). "Performance of Nylon Climbing
Ropes",
http://class.et.byu.edu/mfg340/qualityreports/nylon.htm

Description: Results of a 2^4 factorial experiment measuring the amount
of pressure in pounds needed to have climbing ropes fail. Four factors:
Abrasion, Dirt, Fatigue, Soaked (all Present/Absent). Two replicates
ber combination of factor levels.

Variabls/Columns
Run #   7-8
Abrasion  16  /* 1=Yes, 0=No  */
Dirt   24
Fatigue   32
Soaked   40
Pressure to failure (pounds)  46-48
