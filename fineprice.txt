dataset: fineprice.dat

Source: U. Gneezy and A. Rustichini (2000). "A Fine is a Price," Journal
of Legal Studies, Vol. XXIX, pp. 1-17.

Description: Effects of Parents showing up late at child care centers
in Israel before and after late pick-up fee program is established.
Factors include:
Day Care Center (1-10)
Treatment (late Fee Policy established or not, DCC1-6=trt, DCC7-10, control)
Week  (1-20, Treatment began for Treatment group at week 5, ended at week 16)
Block (5 Blocks of weeks: 1-4,5-8,9-12,13-16,17-20)
Number of children in day care center
Number of parents arriving late
Arcsin of sqrt(proportion of late parents that week)

Trt          16  /* 1=Late fee policy, 2=Control  */
