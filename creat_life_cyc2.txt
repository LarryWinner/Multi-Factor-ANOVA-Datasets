Dataset: creat_life_cyc2.csv 

Sources: 
D.W. Galenson (2003). "Literary Life Cycles: The Careers of American Poets."
Working Paper 9856, National Bureau of Economic Research.


D.W. Galenson (2004). "A Portrait of the Artist as a Young or Old
Innovator: Measuring the Careers of Modern Novelists."
Working Paper 10213, National Bureau of Economic Research.

D.W. Galenson and J. Kotin (2005). "Filming Images or Filming Reality:
The Life Cycles of Movie Directors from D.W. Griffith to Federico Fellini,"
Working Paper 11486, National Bureau of Economic Research.

Description: Age at peak of career for 36 creativists classified as
novelists, poets, and film directors and by their style: 
Conceptualists(finders)/experimentalists(seekers).
Criteria:
Poets: Median age for their 5 most re-printed poems.
Novelists: Median age at publishing for their 3 most important novels
Film Directors: Median age for their films with highest film guide ratings

Variable names
creator   
crType     /* 1= Poet, 2=Novelist, 3=Film Director  */
Style       /* 1=Conceptualist, 2=Experimentalist  */
XA1        /* 1 if Poet, 0 if Nov, -1 if Film */
XA2       /* 1 if Nov, 0 if Poet, -1 if Film  */
XB1       /* 1 if Conceptualist, -1 if Experimental  */
XAB11    /* XA1*XB1  */
XAB21    /* XA2*XB1   */
agePeak
