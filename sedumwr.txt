Dataset: sedumwr.dat

Source: J. Gurevitch and S. Chester (1986). "Analysis of Repeated
Measures Experiments," Ecology, 67(1), pp. 251-255

Description:
 Relative CO2 exchange (change in C02/plant fresh mass) for
 sedum wrightii plants under 2 conditions at 4 time points (3 replicates
 per treatment).

 Variables/Columns
 Treatment  8  /* 1=Dry (water witheld for several weeks) 2=Wet  */
 Replicate (within treatment)   16
 Time Point  24  (2-hour intervals)
 Relative C02 exchange   27-32  /* Negative = output, Positive = uptake  */
