Dataset:   facebook_friend.csv

Source: S.S. Wang, S-I. Moon, K.H. Kwon, C.A. Evans, M.A. Stefanone (2010).
"Face-Off: Implications of Visual Cues on Initiating Friendship on Facebook,"
Computers in Human Behavior, Vol. 26, pp. 226-234.

Description: Results of a study looking at various factors on a person's
      willingness to "friend" a potental facebook profile owner.
Response: Willingness to friend the profile owner (Original Likert scale 1-7, data generated to be > 0., match mean, sd)
Factors:
Evaluator (1=Male, 2=Female)
Profile Owner (1=Male, 2=Female)
Genders (1=Same, 0=Opposite)
Profile picture  (1=Attractive, 2=Unattractive, 3=No picture)

Variable names
grp.cell (1-12) - cell of 2x3x2 Table
gen.prof (1=M, 2=F)  - Gender of Profile owner
gen.eval (1=M, 2=F)  - Gender of Evaluator
picture (1=Att, 2=Unatt, 3=No Pic)
gen.same (1=Yes, 0=No)
willFrnd  - Willingness to Friend the Profile Owner
