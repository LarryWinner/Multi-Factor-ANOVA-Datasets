Dataset: outer_inner_layer.dat

Source: H. Wu and J. Fan (2008). "Measurement of Moisture Transport
with Multi-Layer Clothing Assemblis Consisting of Different Types
of Batting: A Factorial Design Analysis," Textile Research Journal,
Vol. 78 (11): 988-995

Description: Experimental Results with 3 outer layers (Wool, Polyester,
Down) and 3 inner layers (Wool, Polyester, Down). Response
is moisture accumulation (grams). 2 reps/treatment.

Variables/Columns
Outer Layer   8   /* 1=W, 2=P, 3=D  */
Inner Layer   16   /* 1=W, 2=P, 3=D  */
Water accumulation   18-24
