Source: A.H.N. Mousa(1978). "Analysis of the Effect of Size Formulation
Variations on Yarn Tenacity and Elongation," Textile Research Journal,

Description: Factorial experiment relating yarn strength and elongation to
4 factors: Corn Starch, nordux, Tylose, and Trifix.

Method.
