Dataset:  barley_h2o.dat

Source: J.L. Molina-Cano, T. Ramo, et al. (1995). "Effect of Grain Composition

Water Uptake by Malting Barley: A Genetic and Environmental Study," Journal of
the Institute of Brewing, Vol. 101, #2, pp. 79-83.

Description: Water uptaake in barley for 2 genotypes, 2 sites, 2 years
for various periods of steeping time. 2 reps per trt combination.

Variables/Columns

Site     16    /*  1=Bell-lloc, Spain;  2= Dundee.Scotland  */
Year    23-24   /*  91=1991,  92=1992  */
Replicate   32
Steeping Time (Hours)  39-40
Weight of sample (grams)    42-48
