Dataset: marih_beam.dat

Source: B.B. Robinson and J.R. Matchett (1940). "Maihuana Investigations III:
The Effect of Region of Growth of Hemp on Response to the Acid and
Alkaline Beam Tests," Journal of The American Pharmaceutical Association,
Vol.29,Issue 10, pp.448-453

Description:Results of alkaine and Acid Beam Tests on Marijuana plants
of 4 varieties (1=Manchurian "A",2=Manchurian "B",3=African "C", 
4=Kentucky "D") in 4 states (1=Virginia,2=Wisconsin,3=Nebraska,4=Mississippi)
at 3 time points. Plants were grown at 4 blocks within each state.
Response = Total numerical response of 5 separate plant tests for
each plot (block) of a variety for a location (state) for a given 
time period.

Variables/Columns
Variety   8
Time Period    16
State      24
Block      31-32
Alkaline Beam Test Response   39-40
Acid Beam Test Response       47-48
