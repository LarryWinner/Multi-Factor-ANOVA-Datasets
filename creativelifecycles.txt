Datasets: creativelifecycles.dat, creativelifecycles.csv

Sources: 
D.K. Simonson (2007). "Creative Life Cycles in Literature: Poets vs Novelists
or Conceptualists versus Experimentalists?," Psychology of Aesthetics,
Creativity, and the Arts, Vol. 1, #3, pp. 133-139

D.W. Galenson (2003). "Literary Life Cycles: The Careers of American Poets."
Working Paper 9856, National Bureau of Economic Research.


D.W. Galenson (2004). "A Portrait of the Artist as a Young or Old
Innovator: Measuring the Careers of Modern Novelists."
Working Paper 10213, National Bureau of Economic Research.


Description: Age at peak of career for 23 writers classified as
novelist/poet and as Conceptualists(finders)/experimentalists(seekers).
Data in Simonson has one misclassification (Williams as conceptualist)
and one ommission (Dickens age at peak). Using corrected data and assigning
Wilbur death in 2007, reproduces Regression results (using centered age at 
death).

Variables/Columns
author   1-20
Type   28  /* 1= Novelist,  0=Poet  */
Style          36   /* 1=Experimentalist, 0=Conceptualist  */
ageDth   42-44  /* age at Death  */
cageDth  (Centered age at death)    46-52
agePeak (Age at career peak)     59-60
