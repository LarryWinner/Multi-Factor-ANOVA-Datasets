Dataset:  lacrosse.dat

Source: S.V. Caswell, R.G. Deivert (2002). "Lacrosse Helmet Designs and
the Effects of Impact Forces", Journal of Athletic Training, 37(2):164-171.

Description: Simulated data matching
Summary statistics of comparison of 4 models of lacrosse
helmets wrt front and rear impact. Measurements are Gadd Severity index 
scores. Higher values imply more severe impact.

Variables/Columns

Model 8  /* 1=SHC, 2=SHCAF, 3=SHCUL, 4=BUL   */ 
Side  16  /*  1=Front, 2=Back  */ 
Gadd severity score  23-30
