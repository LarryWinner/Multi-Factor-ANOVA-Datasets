Dataset:  vision_measure2.dat

Source: K.D. Majeske (2012). "Approving Vision-Based Measurement Systems
in the Presence of Within-Part Variation," Quality Engineering, Vol. 24,
pp. 49-59.

Description: Experiment with 10 parts being measured at 4 locations, with
each location being measured 4 times (replicates, not used as a factor).
Note that locations are considered to be nested in part.

Variables/Columns
Part Number    7-8
Location       16
Replicate      24
Measurement   26-32
