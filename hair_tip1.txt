Dataset: hair_tip1.csv

Source: N. Gueguen (2012). "Hair Color and Wages: Waitresses with Blond Hair Have More Fun,"
The Journal of Socio-Economics, Vol. 41, pp. 370-372.

Description: Waitress hair color (1=Blond, 2=Black, 3=Brown, 4=Red),
customer gender (1=Male, 2=Female), and tip in Euros among tippers in restaurant in France
(mandatory 12% base tip in France). Data generated to match means and SDs

Variable Names
ID
cust.gend    (1=Male, 2=Female)
hair.color   (1=Blond, 2=Black, 3=Brown, 4=Red)
tip  (Euros)
