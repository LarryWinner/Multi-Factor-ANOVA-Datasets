Dataset:  drumstick.csv

Source: M.I. Aksu, H. Imik, M. Karoglu (2007)> "Influence of Dietary Sorghum
(Sorghum vulgare) and Corn Supplemented with Methionine on Cut-Up Pieces
Weights of Broiler Carcass and Quality Properties of Breast and Drumsticks
Meat," Food Science and Technology International, Vol. 13, #5, pp. 361-367.

Description: Weight of Drumsticks for Broiler Chickens in CRD with 4 diets:
1=Basal Sorghum (BS) 2=Basals Sorghum+Methionine (BSM)
3=Basal Corn (BC)    4=Basal Corn+Methionine (BCM)
Data simulated to perseve treatment means and SDs

Variables Names
diet    (1=BS, 2=BSM, 3=BC,4= BCM)
base    (1=Sorghum, 2=Corn)
methionine     (1=Absent, 2=Present)
drumStkWt (grams)
