Dataset:  mastersf.dat

Source:  www.masters.org

Description: Scores in the Masters golf tournament (unit=round) for
all golfers completing all 4 rounds in the tournament (excludes 
rounds among withdrawals, disqualifications, and those missing
the cut). 1934-2004. No tourney held in 1943-1945.

Variables/Columns
golfer 1-33
golfer id  36-38
year       42-45
round      49
score      53-54
