Dataset:  swamp1.dat

Source: K.C. Ewel and L.P. Wickenheiser (1988). "Effect of Swamp Size
on Growth Rates of Cypress (Taxodium Distichum) Trees," American Midland
Naturalist, Vol. 120, #2, pp.362-370

Description: Water depths at 27 locations within each of 9
swamps (3 Large, 3 medium, 3 small) in Florida. Data
simulated to match published results.

Variables/Columns
Swamp Size    8   /* 1=Small, 2=Medium, 3=Large  */
Swamp Number   16  /* 1:3=Small, 4:6=Med, 7:9=Large  */
Water Level  19:24
