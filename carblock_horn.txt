Dataset: carblock_horn.csv

Source: N. Gueguen, C. Jacob, M. Lourel, A. Pascual (2012). 
"When Drivers See Red: Car Color Frustrators and Drivers' Aggressiveness,"
Aggressive Behavior, Vol. 38, pp. 166-169.

Description: Results of an experiment to observe the effect of blocking car's color
on motorists reaction at blocked light signal (after light has turned green).
Blocking car color: 1=Red, 2=Blue, 3=Green, 4=Black, 5=White
Blocked Motorist Gender: 1=Male, 2=Female
Responses: 1) Time until blocked motorist either honks or blink lights (sec)  2) 1 if Honk, 0 if Blink
Data generated to match means, SDs, and proportions by Color/Gender
Responses should be treated independently.

Variables:
respTime    (Time to Honk or Blink in seconds)
carColor  (1=Red, 2=Blue, 3=Green, 4=Black, 5=White)
gender    (1=Male, 2=Female)
honk      (1 if Honk, 0 if Blink)
