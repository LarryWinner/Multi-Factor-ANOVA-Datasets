Dataset:  karate_board.dat

Source: P.K. Smith, T. Niiler, and P.W. McCullough (2010). "Evaluating
Makiwara Punching Board Performance," Journal of Asian Martial Arts,
Vol 19, #2, pp. 34-45.

Description: Results of experiments measuring deflection (mm) of
makiwara boards of two types (stacked and tapered) and of four
wood types (Cherry, Ash, Fir, and Oak). Unbalanced due to broken
boards.

Variables/Columns
Treatment     8
Wood Type    16     /* 1=Cherry, 2=Ash, 3=Fir, 4=Oak  */
Board Type   24     /* 1=Stacked, 2=Tapered  */
Board (trt) id  26-32
Deflection (mm) 34-40
