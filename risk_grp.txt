Dataset: risk_grp.dat

Source: R.E. Knox and R.K. Safford (1976). "Group Caution at the Race Track," 
Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, Vol.12, pp.317-324

Description: Results of wagers based on individuals and as groups of 4
at a race track. Due to methodological history, analyses of individual
bets were made on both the mean and median of individuals for the group.
Factors:
Risk   (Individual wager versus Group wager)
Order  (Individual/Group   versus    Group/Individual)
Group  (18 groups made up of 4 subjects each)
All groups got each Risk condition,  9 for each ordering.

Variables/Columns
Group            7-8    /* Nested under order  */
Risk Condition    16  /* 1=Individual, 2=Group  */
Order            24  /*  1=I/G,   2=G/I  */
Response1      26-32     /* Wager, with Individual=Mean   */
Response2      34-40    /* wager, with Individual=Median  */
