Dataset: oj_flavonoid.csv

Source: J. Vanamala, L. Reddivari, K.S. Yoo, L.M. Pike, B.S. Patil (2006). 
"Variation in the Content of Bioactive Flavonoids in Different Brands of
Orange and Grapefruit Juices," Journal of Food Composition and Analysis,
Vol. 19, pp. 157-166

Description: 
Total flavonoids (mg/100 mL) for 12 brands of made-from-concentrate orange juice (3 reps per brand)
and 14 brands of not made from concentrate orange juice (3 reps per brand)

7=Minute Maid Country Style   8=MM Home Squeezed + Calcium    9=MM Premium Original
10=MM PO + Calcium   11=MM Premium Extra Vitamins C and E + Zinc   12=Schepps

Not Made from Concentrate:
13=Albertson's Country Style   14=Albertsons OJ + Calcium    15=AvoClassic    16=Florida Natural
17=HEB  Calcium   18=HEB Groves Choice   19=HEB Home Squeezed Style  20=HEB Original
21=Horizon  22=Kroger Homestyle   23=Sams Choice    24=Sams Choice with Calcium
25=Tropicana Pure Premium Calcium   26=Tropicana Pure Premium Low Acid
