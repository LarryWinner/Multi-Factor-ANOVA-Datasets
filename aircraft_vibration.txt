Dataset: aircraft_vibration.csv

Source: M. Zanatta, F.G. Amaral, C.P. Giacomello (2021). "Exposure of Agricultural Pilots to Occupational
Whole-Body Vibration: The Effects of Runway Maintenance and the Stages of Flight," 
International Journal of Industial Ergonomics, Vol. 81. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ergon.2020.103075

Description:
Study of whole-body vibration on pilots with 3 factors:
Aircraft: 1=EMB-201A, 2=EMB-202, 3=A188B, 4=AT402B
Maintenance: 1 = International Roughness Index (IRI) > 8 m/km, 2 = IRI < 8 m/km  
Stage of Flight: 1=Loading, 2=Take-off, 3=Crop-Dusting, 4=Landing
Response: Vibration Total Value 

Variable Names:
aircraft
maintenance
fltStage     (Flight Stage)
vibration
