Dataset: woolfrict1.dat

Source: J. Lindberg(1953). "Relationship Between Various Surface Properties
of Wool Fibers: Part II: Frictional Properties," Textile Research Journal, 
Vol. 23, pp. 225-237.

Description: Results of Experiment measuring top shrinkage under 3 
treatments (untreated, ether-extracted, ether/alcohol extracted),
measured at 3 levels of pH (2, 6, 10),
over 3 experimental runs (nested within treatments). 

Variables/Columns
Run    8
Treatment     16   /* 1=untrt, 2=ether, 3=ether/alcohol  */
pH level   24
Top Shrinkage     29-32
