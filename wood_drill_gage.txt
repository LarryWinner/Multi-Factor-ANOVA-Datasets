Dataset: wood_drill_gage.csv

Source: M-H. C. Li and A. Al-Refaie (2008). "Improving Wooden Parts'
Quality by Adopting DMAIC Procedure," Quality and Reliability Engineering
International, Vol. 24, pp. 351-360.

Description: Gage Repeatability and Peproducibility (GR&R) experiment
on output from a drilling machine process. 2-Way random effects model
with 10 parts, being measured by 3 Operators, on 3 occasions (replicates).
Measurements in mm.

Variables/Names
Part ID  (Part)
Operator ID (Operator)
Replicate (Rep)
Measurement  (Ymeas)
