Dataset: wood_stain.csv

Source: K.J. Potcner and S.M. Kowalski (2004). "How to Analyze a 
Split-Plot Experiment," Quality Progress, Vol. 37, #12, pp.67-74.

Description: Data from a split-plot experiment with 1 whole-plot
factor (pre-tratment, 2 levels), and 1 sub-plot factor (wood stain, 4 levels).
Run as a Completely Randomized design, with Water Resistance as response.

Variables/Labels:
Water Resistance  (Resistance)
Whole-Plot Unit  (WP_Unit)
Pretreatment  (Pretreat)
Stain         (Stain)
