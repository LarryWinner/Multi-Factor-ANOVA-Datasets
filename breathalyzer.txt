Dataset:  breathalyzer.csv

Source: R.G. Gullberg (2008). "Employing Components-of-Variance to Evaluate Rorensic
Breath Test Instruments," Science and Justice, Vol. 48, pp. 2-7

Description: Experiment with 6 Breath analyzers (fixed effect) and 3 subjects (random
effect), with 10 replicates per subject. Data simulated to approximate results in
article.
Machines: 1=Intox 8000, 2=Datamaster, 3=Alcosensor FST PBT, 4=Alcotest 6510 PBT,
5=Alcotest 7110 IR, 6=Datamaster CDM

machine    
mubject    
repNum     
bloodAlc
