Dataset: shirt_tip1.csv

Source: N. Gueguen and C. Jacob (2014). "Clothing Color and Tipping:
Gentlemen Patrons Give More Tips to Waitresses with Red Clothes,"
Journal of Hospitality & Tourism Research, Vol. 38, #2, pp. 275-280.

Description: Waitress shirt color (Black, White, Red, Yellow, Green, Blue),
customer gender (1=Male, 2=Female), and tip in Euros among tippers in restaurant in France
(mandatory 12% base tip in France). Data generated to match means and SDs
 

Variable Names
ID
cust.gend    (1=Male, 2=Female)
shirt.color   (1=Black, 2=White, 3=Red, 4=Yellow, 5=Blue, 6=Green)
tip  (Euros)
