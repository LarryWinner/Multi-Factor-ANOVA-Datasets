Dataset:  sensory_assessor.dat

Source: P.M. Brockhoff and I.M. Skovgaard (1994). "Modelling Individual
Differences Between Assessors in Sensory Evaluation," Food Quality and
Preverence, Vol. 5, pp. 215-224

Description: Results of experiment where 8 assessors rated each of 5 products 
4 times. Ratings were on a 0-15 visual analog scale.

Variables/Columns
Assessor    8
Product    16
Rating    20-24
